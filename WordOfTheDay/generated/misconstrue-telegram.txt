🔹 Word: misconstrue
🔊 Primary pronunciation: /ˌmɪskənˈstruː/

🧠 Meaning 1
Part of speech: transitive verb
Pronunciation: /ˌmɪskənˈstruː/
Definition: to understand or interpret the meaning, intention, or importance of something incorrectly

Examples:

1. Some readers misconstrued the author's criticism as a rejection of the entire theory.
2. The findings should not be misconstrued as proof that the treatment works in every case.
3. She worried that her silence might be misconstrued as a lack of interest.
