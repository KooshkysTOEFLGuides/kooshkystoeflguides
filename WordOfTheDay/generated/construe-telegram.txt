🔹 Word: construe
🔊 Primary pronunciation: /kənˈstruː/

🧠 Meaning 1
Part of speech: transitive verb
Pronunciation: /kənˈstruː/
Definition: to understand or interpret the meaning of a statement, action, situation, or piece of information in a particular way

Examples:

1. His silence could be construed as agreement with the proposal.
2. The researchers cautioned that the results should not be construed as proof of a causal relationship.
3. Some employees construed the new policy as an attempt to reduce their independence.
