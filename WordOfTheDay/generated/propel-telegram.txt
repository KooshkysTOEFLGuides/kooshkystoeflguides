🔹 Word: propel
🔊 Primary pronunciation: /prəˈpɛl/

🧠 Meaning 1
Part of speech: transitive verb
Pronunciation: /prəˈpɛl/
Definition: to push, drive, or cause a person or object to move forward or in a particular direction

Examples:

1. Powerful engines propel the aircraft through the air.
2. The squid propels itself through the water by forcing water out of its body.
3. A strong current propelled the boat toward the shore.

🧠 Meaning 2
Part of speech: transitive verb
Pronunciation: /prəˈpɛl/
Definition: to cause a person, organization, idea, or process to advance quickly or reach a new position, level, or situation

Examples:

1. Her discovery propelled her to international recognition.
2. Rapid technological change has propelled the industry into a period of intense competition.
3. Increased demand helped propel the company's growth during its first five years.
