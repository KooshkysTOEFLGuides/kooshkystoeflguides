🔹 Word: innovative
🔊 Primary pronunciation: /ˈɪnəveɪtɪv/

🧠 Meaning 1
Part of speech: adjective
Pronunciation: /ˈɪnəveɪtɪv/
Definition: introducing or using new ideas, methods, or designs in an original and useful way

Examples:

1. The researchers developed an innovative method for removing pollutants from drinking water.
2. The city adopted an innovative approach to reducing traffic in crowded neighborhoods.
3. Innovative teaching practices can encourage students to participate more actively.
