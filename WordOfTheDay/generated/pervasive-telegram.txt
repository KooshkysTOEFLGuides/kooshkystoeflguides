🔹 Word: pervasive
🔊 Primary pronunciation: /pərˈveɪsɪv/

🧠 Meaning 1
Part of speech: adjective
Pronunciation: /pərˈveɪsɪv/
Definition: spreading through or affecting many parts of a place, group, system, or situation

Examples:

1. Smartphones have had a pervasive influence on modern communication.
2. The report describes a pervasive sense of distrust among employees.
3. Air pollution remains a pervasive problem in many large cities.
