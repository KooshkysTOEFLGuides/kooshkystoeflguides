🔹 Word: propriety
🔊 Primary pronunciation: /prəˈpraɪəti/

🧠 Meaning 1
Part of speech: uncountable noun
Pronunciation: /prəˈpraɪəti/
Definition: behavior that is considered morally, socially, or professionally correct and appropriate

Examples:

1. The committee questioned the propriety of allowing the director to approve a contract involving his own company.
2. Employees are expected to observe professional propriety when dealing with confidential information.
3. The debate focused on the propriety of using public funds for the project.

🧠 Meaning 2
Part of speech: plural countable noun
Pronunciation: /prəˈpraɪəti/
Definition: the accepted rules of polite and socially appropriate behavior

Examples:

1. Guests were expected to observe the proprieties of formal diplomatic events.
2. He cared little about social proprieties and often spoke very directly.
3. The novel shows how strictly the proprieties of upper-class society controlled personal behavior.
