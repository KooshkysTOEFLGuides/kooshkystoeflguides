🔹 Word: prophecy
🔊 Primary pronunciation: /ˈprɑːfəsi/

🧠 Meaning 1
Part of speech: countable or uncountable noun
Pronunciation: /ˈprɑːfəsi/
Definition: a statement that says what will happen in the future, especially one believed to come from divine or supernatural knowledge

Examples:

1. According to the ancient prophecy, a period of peace would follow years of conflict.
2. The story centers on a prophecy that the kingdom will eventually fall.
3. Many followers believed that the prophecy had been fulfilled.
