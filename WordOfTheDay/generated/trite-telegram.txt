🔹 Word: trite
🔊 Primary pronunciation: /traɪt/

🧠 Meaning 1
Part of speech: adjective
Pronunciation: /traɪt/
Definition: expressed or used so often that it no longer seems interesting, original, or effective

Examples:

1. The essay relies on trite phrases instead of developing its ideas clearly.
2. His advice to simply follow your dreams sounded trite to the students.
3. The advertisement was criticized for using trite images of success and happiness.
