🔹 Word: ubiquitous
🔊 Primary pronunciation: /juːˈbɪkwətəs/

🧠 Meaning 1
Part of speech: adjective
Pronunciation: /juːˈbɪkwətəs/
Definition: present, found, or appearing almost everywhere

Examples:

1. Smartphones have become ubiquitous in many parts of the world.
2. Plastic is now a ubiquitous material in modern consumer products.
3. Coffee shops are ubiquitous in the city's business districts.
