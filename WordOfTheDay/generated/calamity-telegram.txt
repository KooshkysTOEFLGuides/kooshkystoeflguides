🔹 Word: calamity
🔊 Primary pronunciation: /kəˈlæməti/

🧠 Meaning 1
Part of speech: countable noun
Pronunciation: /kəˈlæməti/
Definition: a terrible event that causes great damage, suffering, or loss

Examples:

1. The earthquake was a major calamity that left thousands of people without homes.
2. Crop failure could become a national calamity if food supplies are already limited.
3. Better warning systems can help communities prepare for natural calamities.
