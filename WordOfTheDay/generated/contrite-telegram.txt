🔹 Word: contrite
🔊 Primary pronunciation: /kənˈtraɪt/

🧠 Meaning 1
Part of speech: adjective
Pronunciation: /kənˈtraɪt/
Definition: feeling and showing sincere guilt or regret because you have done something wrong

Examples:

1. He appeared genuinely contrite after realizing how much his comments had hurt his colleagues.
2. The company issued a contrite apology for failing to protect customers' personal information.
3. She sounded contrite as she admitted that she had ignored the warning signs.
