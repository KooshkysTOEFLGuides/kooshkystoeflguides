🔹 Word: corresponding
🔊 Primary pronunciation: /ˌkɔːrəˈspɑːndɪŋ/

🧠 Meaning 1
Part of speech: adjective
Pronunciation: /ˌkɔːrəˈspɑːndɪŋ/
Definition: directly related to something else, especially because the two things change together, belong together, or have matching positions

Examples:

1. An increase in production was accompanied by a corresponding increase in energy use.
2. Each number on the map refers to a corresponding location in the table.
3. Higher levels of education are often associated with a corresponding rise in average income.

🧠 Meaning 2
Part of speech: adjective
Pronunciation: /ˌkɔːrəˈspɑːndɪŋ/
Definition: having the same or a very similar function, position, or meaning as something in another system, group, or situation

Examples:

1. Researchers compared each region with the corresponding region in the earlier survey.
2. The French term has no exact corresponding expression in English.
3. Students should enter each answer in the corresponding box on the form.
