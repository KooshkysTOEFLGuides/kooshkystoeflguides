🔹 Word: devious
🔊 Primary pronunciation: /ˈdiːviəs/

🧠 Meaning 1
Part of speech: adjective
Pronunciation: /ˈdiːviəs/
Definition: using dishonest, secretive, or clever methods to get what you want

Examples:

1. The manager used devious tactics to hide the true cost of the project.
2. Investigators uncovered a devious scheme to avoid paying taxes.
3. The character is intelligent but devious and frequently manipulates people for his own benefit.

🧠 Meaning 2
Part of speech: adjective
Pronunciation: /ˈdiːviəs/
Definition: indirect and complicated rather than following the simplest or most direct route

Examples:

1. Because the main road was closed, we had to take a devious route through several small villages.
2. The river follows a devious course through the mountains.
3. The hikers reached the valley by a long and devious path.
