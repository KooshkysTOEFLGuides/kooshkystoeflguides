🔹 Word: unequivocally
🔊 Primary pronunciation: /ˌʌnɪˈkwɪvəkəli/

🧠 Meaning 1
Part of speech: adverb
Pronunciation: /ˌʌnɪˈkwɪvəkəli/
Definition: in a completely clear and definite way that leaves no doubt about what is meant, believed, or true

Examples:

1. The researchers stated unequivocally that the evidence did not support the original theory.
2. The university unequivocally rejected the accusation of discrimination.
3. The data show unequivocally that average temperatures have increased over the study period.
