🔹 Word: determine
🔊 Primary pronunciation: /dɪˈtɝːmɪn/

🧠 Meaning 1
Part of speech: transitive verb
Pronunciation: /dɪˈtɝːmɪn/
Definition: to discover, calculate, or establish a fact, amount, cause, or answer through careful examination

Examples:

1. Researchers are trying to determine whether sleep quality affects long-term memory.
2. The laboratory test can determine the concentration of lead in the water.
3. Investigators could not determine the exact cause of the equipment failure.

🧠 Meaning 2
Part of speech: transitive verb
Pronunciation: /dɪˈtɝːmɪn/
Definition: to control, cause, or strongly influence the nature or result of something

Examples:

1. Access to reliable transportation can determine whether workers accept jobs far from home.
2. Soil quality largely determines which crops can grow in a region.
3. A society's institutions help determine how resources are distributed.

🧠 Meaning 3
Part of speech: transitive verb
Pronunciation: /dɪˈtɝːmɪn/
Definition: to make an official or authoritative decision about something after considering the facts

Examples:

1. After reviewing the evidence, the board determined that the investigation should continue.
2. The court will determine the appropriate level of compensation.
3. Officials determined that the building should remain closed until repairs were complete.
