🔹 Word: linger
🔊 Primary pronunciation: /ˈlɪŋɡər/

🧠 Meaning 1
Part of speech: intransitive verb
Pronunciation: /ˈlɪŋɡər/
Definition: to stay somewhere longer than necessary or expected, often because you do not want to leave

Examples:

1. Several students lingered after class to ask the professor additional questions.
2. Visitors often linger in the museum's central hall before moving to the next exhibit.
3. She lingered by the door for a few moments before finally leaving.

🧠 Meaning 2
Part of speech: intransitive verb
Pronunciation: /ˈlɪŋɡər/
Definition: to continue to exist, be noticeable, or have an effect after the original cause or event has ended

Examples:

1. The smell of smoke lingered in the room long after the fire had been extinguished.
2. Doubts about the safety of the product continued to linger among consumers.
3. The effects of the economic crisis lingered for several years.

🧠 Meaning 3
Part of speech: intransitive verb
Pronunciation: /ˈlɪŋɡər/
Definition: to spend more time than usual considering, looking at, or enjoying something

Examples:

1. The report lingers over the causes of the conflict but says little about its consequences.
2. She lingered over her coffee while reading the morning newspaper.
3. The documentary lingers on several images of the damaged landscape.
