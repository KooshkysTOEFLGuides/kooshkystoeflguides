🔹 Word: inventory
🔊 Primary pronunciation: /ˈɪnvənˌtɔːri/

🧠 Meaning 1
Part of speech: countable or uncountable noun
Pronunciation: /ˈɪnvənˌtɔːri/
Definition: the goods, materials, or products that a business or organization has available or stored for future use or sale

Examples:

1. The company reduced its inventory after demand for the product declined.
2. Retailers often increase their inventory before major shopping seasons.
3. The factory keeps enough raw materials in inventory to avoid interruptions in production.

🧠 Meaning 2
Part of speech: countable noun
Pronunciation: /ˈɪnvənˌtɔːri/
Definition: a complete and detailed list of the items, resources, or materials that a person or organization has

Examples:

1. The museum created an inventory of all the artifacts in its collection.
2. Researchers prepared an inventory of plant species found in the protected area.
3. The laboratory maintains a detailed inventory of its chemicals and equipment.

🧠 Meaning 3
Part of speech: transitive verb
Pronunciation: /ˈɪnvənˌtɔːri/
Definition: to make a detailed list of the items or resources in a place or collection

Examples:

1. Museum staff inventoried thousands of objects before moving the collection.
2. Researchers inventoried the plant species found in each study area.
3. The organization inventoried its equipment to determine what needed to be replaced.
