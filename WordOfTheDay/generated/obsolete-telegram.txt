🔹 Word: obsolete
🔊 Primary pronunciation: /ˌɑːbsəˈliːt/

🧠 Meaning 1
Part of speech: adjective
Pronunciation: /ˌɑːbsəˈliːt/
Definition: no longer used, needed, or useful, usually because something newer or more effective has replaced it

Examples:

1. Many older computer systems became obsolete as faster and cheaper technology was developed.
2. The company replaced its obsolete equipment with modern automated machines.
3. Some traditional skills become obsolete when new technologies change the way work is performed.
