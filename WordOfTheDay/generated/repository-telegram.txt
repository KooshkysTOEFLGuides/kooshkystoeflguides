🔹 Word: repository
🔊 Primary pronunciation: /rɪˈpɑːzəˌtɔːri/

🧠 Meaning 1
Part of speech: countable noun
Pronunciation: /rɪˈpɑːzəˌtɔːri/
Definition: a place or system where large amounts of information, documents, objects, or other materials are stored and kept

Examples:

1. The university created a digital repository for research papers produced by its faculty.
2. The museum serves as a repository for thousands of historical documents and artifacts.
3. Scientists stored the genetic data in a central online repository.

🧠 Meaning 2
Part of speech: countable noun
Pronunciation: /rɪˈpɑːzəˌtɔːri/
Definition: a person, book, organization, or other source that contains or preserves a large amount of knowledge or information

Examples:

1. The archive is an important repository of information about the region's history.
2. Traditional stories can serve as a repository of a community's cultural knowledge.
3. For decades, the professor was regarded as a repository of knowledge about ancient languages.
