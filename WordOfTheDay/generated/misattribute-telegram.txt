🔹 Word: misattribute
🔊 Primary pronunciation: /ˌmɪsəˈtrɪbjuːt/

🧠 Meaning 1
Part of speech: transitive verb
Pronunciation: /ˌmɪsəˈtrɪbjuːt/
Definition: to wrongly believe that an event, condition, or result was caused by a particular person, thing, or factor

Examples:

1. Researchers initially misattributed the population decline to disease rather than habitat loss.
2. People sometimes misattribute normal changes in the weather to long-term climate change.
3. The company misattributed the drop in sales to higher prices, but later research showed that poor customer service was the main cause.

🧠 Meaning 2
Part of speech: transitive verb
Pronunciation: /ˌmɪsəˈtrɪbjuːt/
Definition: to wrongly identify a particular person or group as the creator, author, speaker, or source of a work, idea, statement, or achievement

Examples:

1. The quotation is frequently misattributed to Albert Einstein.
2. For many years, scholars misattributed the painting to a more famous artist.
3. Historical inventions are sometimes misattributed to a single individual even when many people contributed to their development.
