🔹 Word: turmoil
🔊 Primary pronunciation: /ˈtɝːmɔɪl/

🧠 Meaning 1
Part of speech: uncountable noun
Pronunciation: /ˈtɝːmɔɪl/
Definition: a state of great confusion, uncertainty, or disorder, especially during a period of serious change or conflict

Examples:

1. The country experienced years of political turmoil after the government collapsed.
2. Financial turmoil caused investors to become much more cautious.
3. The sudden leadership change threw the organization into turmoil.
