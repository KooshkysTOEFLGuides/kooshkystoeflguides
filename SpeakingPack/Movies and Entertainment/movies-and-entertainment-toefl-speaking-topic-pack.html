It also gives residents a shared cultural space and creates jobs in the area. Support should not mean keeping every poorly managed business open permanently. The theater should adapt to audience habits, maintain accessibility, and offer programs that serve the community. Limited public or community support is justified when the venue provides educational and cultural benefits that cannot be measured only through ticket profit.
